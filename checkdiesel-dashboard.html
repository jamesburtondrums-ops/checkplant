Plant machinery diesel use

Estimated litres per engine hour
For PLANT2 machinery, CheckDiesel links each fuel transaction to its most recent CheckPlant daily check (same day or up to two days earlier), then measures hours up to the next refuel. Indicative L/h = litres issued at the next refuel ÷ increase in engine hours. This assumes reasonably complete refuelling; partial fills and missing checks can distort the estimate. Pump ODOMETER readings are not used for PLANT2 plant efficiency.

Transfers, bowsers and branch fuel

Separated from plant consumption
